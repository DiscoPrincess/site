THE DISCOPRINCESS MANIFESTO

For the midnight misfits, the tender rebels, and everyone who refuses to become smaller just to fit the room.

DP / THE AUX ORDER02 / 06

01 / THE PROCLAMATION

Let the ordinary tremble.

Beloved creatures of the late hour, we have spent enough evenings apologising for our brightness. Enough shrinking our laughter, editing our strangeness, and calling our desires inconvenient.

Tonight we establish a different kingdom: a republic of rhythm, music, imagination, kindness and magnificent nerve. Its palace may be a bedroom. Its cathedral may be a pair of headphones. Its royal procession may consist of one person making tea at two in the morning.

DiscoPrincess takes the glittering throne as our mistress of ceremonies: keeper of the unexpected track, patron of the beautifully peculiar, and first defender of the right to change your mind.

Her crown is theatrical. Your freedom is real. No title outranks your boundaries. No chant replaces your judgement. We gather because something in the music says: there you are.

We do not ask to be normal.
We ask for the volume to be turned up.
DP / THE AUX ORDER03 / 06

02 / THE SEVEN ARTICLES

The Aux constitution.

  1. KEEP YOUR OWN CROWN

    Admire the Princess. Keep your own mind. Nobody here is required to agree, adore, or applaud on command.

  2. MAKE ROOM FOR THE STRANGE

    Odd tastes, crooked humour, improbable dreams: bring them. Curiosity is our dress code.

  3. LET KINDNESS HAVE TEETH

    Be warm without becoming a doormat. A clear boundary is a beautiful thing.

  4. HONOUR THE PRIVATE WORLD

    A secret stays a secret. A face, name, message or story belongs to the person who shared it.

  5. WORSHIP THE SONG, NOT THE SCORE

    Create for the shiver, the laugh, the unexpected feeling. Popularity is no proof of magic.

  6. ALLOW THE QUIET ONES TO GLOW

    No performance is owed. Listening counts. Resting counts. Showing up as you are counts.

  7. LEAVE EVERY DOOR UNLOCKED

    Belonging is freely chosen. Come closer, step back, leave, return. Affection never becomes a debt.

03 / RITUALS OF THE ORDER

After dark, we begin.

01 / THE OPENING TRACK

Choose one song that matches your actual mood. No pretending to be cheerful. Let the first minute belong entirely to listening.

02 / THE CORONATION

Put on something that feels like you: an old hoodie, outrageous earrings, imaginary armour. Declare: “I arrive as myself.”

03 / THE MIDNIGHT OFFERING

Bring a song, a drawing, a ridiculous sentence, a photograph you choose to share, or simply your attention. Nothing needs to be impressive.

04 / THE ROYAL PAUSE

When the world becomes too loud, lower the lights and step away. A proper kingdom can survive its citizens taking a nap.

05 / THE LAST LIGHT

Close with one good thing you noticed. Let the final track finish. Send people back into their own lives a little warmer.

04 / THE ROYAL HOUSE

A little sacred nonsense.

The crown stands for self-possession. Wear yours at whatever angle suits the evening. The mirror stands for recognition without comparison. You are allowed to see yourself kindly.
